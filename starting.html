Starting Your Experience

Build a Strong Partnership

Start your experience by establishing clear communication with your partner and agreeing on how your team will work together. Use the steps below to prepare for your first meeting and turn initial ideas into shared expectations.

Steps to Launch Your Project

  1. 01

    Introduce Your Team

    Send your partner a short introduction explaining who is on your team and why you are reaching out. Share your availability and ask to schedule a kickoff meeting.

  2. 02

    Prepare for Your First Meeting

    Create and share an agenda before the meeting. Prepare questions about your partner's goals, intended users, timeline, and expectations. Make time to listen to their priorities.

  3. 03

    Agree on Roles and Scope

    Discuss responsibilities, possible deliverables, and what your team can realistically complete. Record decisions and identify questions that still need answers.

  4. 04

    Establish Communication Practices

    Agree on a primary contact, communication channels, and meeting frequency. Send a follow-up summary confirming decisions, next steps, and who is responsible for each action.

  • Initial Client Email Examples — Use these examples when drafting your first message. Adapt the introduction and meeting request to your team and partner.
  • Initial Client Meeting Sample Agenda — Use this sample to organize your kickoff meeting and prepare questions before you meet.
  • Ginsberg Center Campus-Community Partnerships Toolkit — Explore guidance for building respectful, reciprocal relationships with community partners.

Before Moving Forward

Review these questions with your team. If an answer is unclear, discuss it with your partner before proceeding.

  • Have we agreed on the project goals and deliverables?
  • Does each team member understand their responsibilities?
  • Have we identified our main partner contact?
  • Have we agreed on how and when to communicate?
  • Have we documented our next steps and deadlines?
